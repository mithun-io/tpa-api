Current state: Basic field matching and duplicate checks.
Pro Architect Vision: Implement "Image Forensic Analysis." The AI should detect if a hospital bill has been digitally altered (Photoshop/PDF editing) or if a signature is a "copy-paste."
Why? Fraudulent claims are the biggest loss for TPAs. Detecting metadata changes or pixel inconsistencies can save millions for the Carrier.

3. Medical Coding (ICD-10) & Clinical Validation
Current state: Extracts text but doesn't "understand" medicine.
Pro Architect Vision: Integrate with a Medical Ontology API. If the diagnosis is "Common Cold" but the hospital bill includes "Cardiac Surgery" charges, the system should instantly flag it as a "High Risk/Rejection" case.
Why? This prevents "Up-coding" (hospitals overcharging for minor treatments).

4. Reactive Event-Driven Pipeline (Kafka-First)
Current state: Likely synchronous processing (Wait for OCR -> Wait for Rules).
Pro Architect Vision: Transform the claim flow into a Distributed Event Pipeline using Spring Cloud Stream & Kafka.
Step 1: CLAIM_UPLOADED event.
Step 2: OCR_COMPLETED event.
Step 3: RULE_EVALUATED event.
Why? If the OCR service is slow or down, the system doesn't crash; the claim just waits in a queue and processes automatically when ready.

Pro Architect Vision: Implement SaaS Multi-tenancy. Each Carrier (e.g., Star Health vs. HDFC Ergo) gets their own portal with their own logos, custom SLAs, and specific approval workflows.

6. "Uber-Style" Claim Tracking for Customers

Why? Ensures the TPA meets its contractual Turnaround Time (TAT) with the Carrier.

8. Automated Clearing House (ACH) & Instant Payouts
Current state: System stops at "Approved."
Pro Architect Vision: Integrate with Payment Gateways (IMPS/NEFT/UPI). Upon "Final Approval" by the Admin, the system triggers an API call to the bank to disburse the money to the hospital or customer immediately.
Why? Speed of payment is the #1 metric for customer satisfaction in insurance.

9. Blockchain-Based Immutable Audit Trail
Current state: Standard database logs.
Pro Architect Vision: Store the "Hash" of the claim decision and the documents on a private ledger (Hyperledger).
Why? In legal disputes or regulatory audits, you can prove that the claim was not tampered with after the decision was made. It provides "Non-repudiation."
